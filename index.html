Djamel Bennecib is a French writer, filmmaker, and screenwriting instructor based near Paris. He has written and directed short films selected in festivals, taught screenwriting, and worked as a script reader for production companies.

His work — on screen and on the page — is driven by a single conviction: stories have the power to transform lives.

The books

French

Le Scénario de la Blessure

Les grandes histoires ne sont pas complexes. Elles sont cohérentes. Elles naissent d'une blessure. Une blessure qui façonne une croyance. Une croyance qui crée un défaut. Un défaut qui impose un choix. Et un choix qui transforme un destin.

À travers une méthode claire et structurée, Le Scénario de la Blessure propose une approche exigeante du récit, centrée sur le conflit intérieur comme moteur dramatique.

Rocky. Breaking Bad. Des œuvres simples en apparence. Implacables dans leur mécanique.

Ce livre ne t'apprend pas à ajouter des rebondissements. Il t'apprend à écrire des transformations.

Disponible sur Amazon PDF — Guide complet · 19 €

English

Write from the Wound

The English edition of Le Scénario de la Blessure. Great stories are not complex. They are coherent. They are born from a wound — a wound that shapes a belief, a belief that creates a flaw, a flaw that forces a choice, and a choice that transforms a destiny.

A rigorous, structured approach to character-driven storytelling, built from the analysis of hundreds of scripts for major international platforms.

This book doesn't teach you to add twists. It teaches you to write transformations.

English

AT 36: When Everything Breaks and What It Builds

At 36 years old, something cracks for almost everyone. No matter where you come from, what you do, or what you believe in, something shifts around that age. Careers stall. Marriages falter. Friendships fade. The lives we've built suddenly stop holding.

It can feel like everything is breaking. But sometimes what breaks is also what builds.

Part mirror, part reflection, part quiet reconstruction — through chapters, letters, interludes, and confessions, Djamel Bennecib explores the strange moment when life forces us to face what we've avoided, and begin building something stronger.

"I wrote the book I needed when everything cracked open. Maybe it's the one you need too."

English

The Power of Being Able

Most people spend their lives accumulating things they believe will finally make them feel free. A house. A career. Financial security. The visible markers of a life well built.

Yet something quietly happens when they arrive. The freedom they expected is not there. What was meant to liberate begins to confine.

Because happiness does not come from owning. It comes from being able.

Across ten chapters, Djamel Bennecib reveals why true stability comes not from holding, but from remaining capable. Why freedom is not the absence of structure, but the presence of possibility within it. And why the happiest individuals are not those who possess the most, but those who remain able.
